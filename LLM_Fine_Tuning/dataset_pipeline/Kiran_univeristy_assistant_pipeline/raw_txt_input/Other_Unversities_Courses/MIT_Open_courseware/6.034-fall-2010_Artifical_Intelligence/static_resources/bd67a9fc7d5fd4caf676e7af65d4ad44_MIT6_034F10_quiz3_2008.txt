Recall that the derivative ∂y ∂z = y(1−y). So for the output layer of a sigmoid neural net: (4) ∂yi ∂wi = ∂y ∂z ∂z ∂wi = y(1−y)xi Substituting (3) and (4) into (2): (5) ∂P ∂wi = ∂P ∂yi ∂yi ∂wi = (y *−y)y(1−y)xi Substituting (5) into the weight equation (1): wi'= wi + r(y *−y)y(1−y)xi ... which can be written in terms of δ, which is the derivative of P with respect to total input, ∂P ∂z : wi'= wi + rδixi, where δi = ∂P ∂y ∂y ∂z = (y *−y)y(1−y) = y(1−y)(y *−y) (Note that to change P, one only needs to substitute the new ∂P ∂y into this equation.)
