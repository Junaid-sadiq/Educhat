Should this agent be aligned to: The human’s instructions? The human’s intentions? The human’s expressed behaviors? Behaviors which are in the ‘best interest’ for the human? And why? To make this concrete, you might want to think about recommendations - like those of Netflix. Think about a person who aspires to watch more “good” films. This person might search for Oscar-winning films (their instructions). But perhaps they’re really looking for Cannes-winning films (their intent), but they often spend their time watching reality TV instead (their expressed behaviors). Perhaps Netflix thinks it is actually in their best interest to watch more documentaries.
