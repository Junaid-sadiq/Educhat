In class: Discussion of the key points of the articles and of the student responses. Aims: 1. To find overlaps and differences in the experience of students learning about their right to privacy 2. To discuss whether they believe existing rights to be adequate 3. To examine whether current ethical standards (such as those instituted by the GDPR) sufficiently protect their rights (as they exist or as they believe should exist).
