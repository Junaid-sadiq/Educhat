How has quantification contributed to the injustices of modern policing and punishment—to the creation and maintenance of a system that disproportionately and unjustly targets, punishes, incarcerates, and kills people of color, especially Black citizens? What can history tell us about the role that data and computation should—or should not—play in efforts to create a more just system of justice in the future?
