CIE color matching 59 • If I have a given spectrum X • I compute its response to the 3 matching curves (multiply and integrate) • I use these 3 responses to scale my 3 primaries (435.8, 546.1 and 700nm) • I get a metamer of X (perfect color reproduction) CIE color matching: what does it mean? 60 Relation to cone curves • Project to the same subspace – b, g, and r are linear combinations of S, M and L • Related by 3x3 matrix. • Unfortunately unknown at that time.
