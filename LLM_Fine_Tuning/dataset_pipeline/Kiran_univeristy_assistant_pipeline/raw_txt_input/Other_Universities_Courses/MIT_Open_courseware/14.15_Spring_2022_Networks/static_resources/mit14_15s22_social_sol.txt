Are all such equilibria equally resistant to manipulation? Loosely, the game of choosing weights θi resembles a coordination game where agents in the network want to mimic the weights of others. When agents in society coordinate on whether to listen to the crowd (sheep) or form their own opinions from news (narcissists), they are resistant to manipulation. However, a mix of sheepish and narcissistic behavior is also an equilibrium that provides the perfect breeding ground for the principal to spread disinformation.
