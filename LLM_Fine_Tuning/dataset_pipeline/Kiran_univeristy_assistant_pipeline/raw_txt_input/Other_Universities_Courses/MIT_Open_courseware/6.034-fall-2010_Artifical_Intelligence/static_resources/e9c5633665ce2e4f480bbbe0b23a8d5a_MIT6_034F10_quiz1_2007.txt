If no matching assertion is found, the backward chainer tries to ﬁnd a rule with a matching consequent. In case none are found, then the backward chainer assumes the hypothesis is false. 2) The backward chainer never alters the database, so it can derive the same result multiple times. 3) Rules are tried in the order they appear. 4) Antecedents are tried in the order they appear.
