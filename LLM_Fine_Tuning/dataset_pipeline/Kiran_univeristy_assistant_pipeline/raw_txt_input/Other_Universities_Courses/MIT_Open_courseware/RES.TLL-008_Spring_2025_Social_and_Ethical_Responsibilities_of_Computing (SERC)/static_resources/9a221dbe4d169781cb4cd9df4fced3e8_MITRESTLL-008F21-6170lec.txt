Choice: Users post their COVID-19 Status by acquiring an authorization code from their doctor. ● This prevents bad actors from manipulating the system, forcing unnecessary self-isolation, unlike without requiring authorization codes. ● Users consent, unlike with the voice monitoring solution. ● Fewer users report, since this requires the financial means/feelings of security needed to see a doctor in the US. ● Privacy is compromised since users can be associated with a specific doctor’s visit. 68 Contact Tracing Choose & Justify 5 minutes 69 1.
